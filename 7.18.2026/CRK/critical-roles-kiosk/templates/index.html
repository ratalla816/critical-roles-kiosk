Available Roles

Find Your Fit

Problem Solve

Identify damaged shipping containers and repackage customer orders. Replace missing labels, prepare unsellable products, and process replacement orders for damaged or incorrect items.

Learning Ambassador

Serve as a key touchpoint between Learning and Operations. Guide onboarding and develop associates through personalized training. A voluntary development opportunity — complement to your current role.

GTDR Certification

Serve as gatekeeper of associate safety while docking and undocking commercial vehicles. One of Amazon's critical safety tasks — only certified associates may perform it. Uses radio and Fire tablet for pre-dock checks.

Sort Slide

A manual sort slide uses divert arms to push packages down a large metal chute with divided lanes. Associates at the base sort packages into one of four off-chutes.

Crossdock

Fast-paced and exciting — receive packages from other Amazon sites and send them right back out to AMZL partners. No sorting or container building. Just load and unload go-carts, pallets, and shuttles.

Shuttle Dump

Inbound equipment used to induct volume from shuttles by tipping them at an angle — packages spill into a chute feeding the take-away conveyor. Load shuttles, unload empties, operate equipment, and clear jams.

Fluid Unloader

Unload floor loaded packages onto a telescopic conveyor from inside the trailer.

Yep — you get to wear a hard hat. 🪖

Waterspider

Close containers physically and virtually · Print labels · Open new containers · Stage containers to the buffer zone. The connective tissue of a smooth sort operation.

Smalls

Small boxes and jiffies make up over 50% of MCI5's sortable volume — our bread and butter. Fast-paced, packages are light, and no pallet wrapping required. What are you waiting for?

Flow Scanner

Do you wanna go FAST? High-volume locations segregated from regular WIP — scan containers at rates many times higher than standard builders. Just scan the stacking filter and go.

Expected rate: 1,000 units/hour.

Container Stager / Loader

Move closed pallets and carts from the buffer zone into staging areas for DDU drivers and Container Loaders to load onto outbound trucks. Critical link in the outbound chain.
